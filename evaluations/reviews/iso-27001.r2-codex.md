# iso-27001.md — review r2-codex (2026-05-28)

Findings

- Invented artifacts `AMENDMENT` and `recourse` · High · Mapping row “Nonconformity + corrective action”; Mermaid `IM` edge · Replace with canonical `POLICY` update/versioning plus corrective `ENGAGEMENT`, authorized by `MANDATE`/`AUTHORITY` and evidenced by `SIGNATURE`.

- Scope appears to produce signed evidence · High · Mermaid `H["h2a scope"] --> "signed journal..."` · Do not make scope the signer/source. Route evidence from scoped `ENGAGEMENT`/`POLICY`/`CONTRACT` artifacts signed by `PRINCIPAL`/`EXECUTIF`/authorized roles.

- “Risk owner = mandated PRINCIPAL” violates authority model · High · Mapping row “Risk assessment & treatment” · A `PRINCIPAL` owns/grounds the scope; it is not “mandated.” Say risk ownership belongs to the scope’s `PRINCIPAL`, with execution delegated by `MANDATE` to `EXECUTIF`/`MANDATAIRE` as needed.

- Escalation target is underspecified · Medium · `ENFORCEMENT_PLAN` descriptions · State that nonconformity escalation targets the scope’s competent authority, normally the scoped `PRINCIPAL` or delegated `EXECUTIF`.

- Certification body modeled as imposing authority · High · “Nearest built-in profile + delta”; Compatibility hypothesis · A certification body audits conformity; it does not impose the ISMS policy. Use external `CONTROL` for the certifier, and reserve external `AUTHORITY` for a regulator/customer/legal source that actually imposes requirements.

- Clause 7 support is under-mapped · Medium · Mermaid and mapping table · Add explicit support/documented-information coverage: competence, awareness, communication are mostly external; documented information can map to signed/versioned artifacts and disclosure.

- Management review is conflated with audit/control · Medium · Mermaid `PE["internal audit + mgmt review"] --> CONTROL` · Split cl.9.2 internal audit to `CONTROL`; map cl.9.3 management review to `EXECUTIF` under the scope authority.

- Coverage marks overclaim h2a enforcement · Medium · Rows for operation, monitoring/internal audit, nonconformity · Downgrade several ✅ to `~`: h2a records, schedules, signs, and discloses evidence, but does not perform control operation, audit judgment, effectiveness testing, or corrective-action validation.

- SoA as `CONTRACT` is weak · Medium · Mapping row “Statement of Applicability”; Contracts vs policies · Model SoA primarily as `POLICY`; use `CONTRACT` only when a customer/regulator agreement actually creates bilateral obligations.

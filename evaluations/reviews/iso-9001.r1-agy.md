# iso-9001.md — review r1-agy (2026-05-28)

Here is the expert review of the `evaluations/iso-9001.md` document against the h2a protocol evaluation library rules, invariants, and ISO standards.

### Findings

* **Process Owner Role Inversion** · **High** · Mapping Table (Process approach cl. 4.4) · A `PRINCIPAL` owns the engagement/resource and is never "mandated." Change "process owner = mandated PRINCIPAL" to "process owner = PRINCIPAL (who owns the process engagement and mandates its execution to a `MANDATAIRE`/`AGENT` role)."
* **Conflation of Management Review with CONTROL** · **Medium** · Mermaid Diagram (`IA` node & flow) · Management review is an `EXECUTIF` function, not a `CONTROL` function. Split the `IA` node into two separate nodes: `IA["internal audit (cl. 9.1-9.2)"]` (flows via `CONTROL`) and `MR["management review (cl. 9.3)"]` (flows via `EXECUTIF`).
* **Misidentification of Certification Body Role** · **High** · "Certification common questions" (Q5) & "Compatibility hypothesis" · The certification body is an external `CONTROL` that audits; the standard/regulator is the external `AUTHORITY` that imposes the requirement. Correct the text which calls the certification body an "external certification AUTHORITY."
* **Scope Implied as a Signing Entity** · **Medium** · Mermaid Diagram (`H["h2a scope"] --> AUD` transition) · A scope itself never signs; only roles and artifacts sign. Revise the diagram flow or label to clarify that the signed journal/attestations originate from roles/artifacts *within* the scope, rather than being signed by the scope itself.
* **Under-specified Escalation Target** · **Low** · Mapping Table (Nonconformity cl. 10.2) · Recourse and escalation must target the competent authority of the scope. Add a note that `recourse` (escalation) targets the scope's competent `AUTHORITY`.
* **Missing Explicit Scope Association** · **Low** · "Contracts vs policies" (ENGAGEMENT bullet) · An `ENGAGEMENT` must always have a defined scope. Explicitly state that each process `ENGAGEMENT` is bound to and executed within a specific `scope`.

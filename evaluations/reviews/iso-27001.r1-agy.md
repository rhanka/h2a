Here is the expert review of the `evaluations/iso-27001.md` draft against the evaluation rubric:

### Findings

* **Invented h2a artifact (`AMENDMENT`)** · **severity:** High · **location:** Mapping table (row 9, "Nonconformity + corrective action") and Mermaid diagram (`IM --> recourse + amendment + journal`) · **suggested fix:** Remove the invented term `AMENDMENT` (which is not in the canonical list of artifacts). Represent corrective changes via canonical primitives, such as an updated/versioned `POLICY` or a remedial `ENGAGEMENT`.
* **Scope cryptographically signing evidence** · **severity:** High · **location:** Mermaid diagram (`H["h2a scope (first-class)"] -->|"evidence: signed journal..."| AUD`) · **suggested fix:** Invariant violation: a scope is a passive structural container/context and never signs. Reroute the arrow to originate from the active roles (`EXECUTIF` / `CONTROL`) or signed artifacts (`ENGAGEMENT` / `CONTRACT` journals) within the scope that actually perform the signing and disclosure.
* **Contradictory "mandated PRINCIPAL" concept** · **severity:** Medium · **location:** Mapping table (row 3, "Risk assessment & treatment, risk owners") · **suggested fix:** Invariant violation: in h2a, a `PRINCIPAL` is the root authority/owner of a scope and grants mandates; they are never "mandated" (which describes a `MANDATAIRE` or `EXECUTIF` executing a mandate). Clarify that the risk owner is a `PRINCIPAL` who owns the risk and may delegate operational execution via a `MANDATE`.
* **Missing escalation target** · **severity:** Medium · **location:** Mapping table (row 3) and "Contracts vs policies" section (`ENFORCEMENT_PLAN` description) · **suggested fix:** Invariant violation: escalation must target the scope's competent authority. Explicitly state that under the `ENFORCEMENT_PLAN`, nonconformity escalations target the scope's `PRINCIPAL` or `EXECUTIF`.
* **Conflation of SoA as a CONTRACT** · **severity:** Medium · **location:** Mapping table (row 4, "Statement of Applicability") and "Contracts vs policies" section · **suggested fix:** A Statement of Applicability is the organization's unilateral declaration of controls and justifications. Modeling it as a `CONTRACT` (which implies a bilateral or multilateral agreement that spawns engagements) is incorrect. Model the SoA strictly as a `POLICY` (or a set of versioned policies with `adoptionMode: ratified` or `imposed`).
* **Management Review role mismatch in diagram** · **severity:** Low-Medium · **location:** Mermaid diagram (`PE["internal audit + mgmt review"] --> CONTROL role`) vs Mapping table (row 8, "Management review") · **suggested fix:** Management review is an executive function carried out by top management (`EXECUTIF`), not an audit function carried out by `CONTROL`. Separate Management Review from Internal Audit in the diagram, mapping `cl.9.3` to the `EXECUTIF` role rather than the `CONTROL` role.
* **Omission of Clause 7 (Support)** · **severity:** Low · **location:** Mapping table and Mermaid diagram · **suggested fix:** Clause 7 (specifically cl.7.5 Documented Information) is crucial to the journal-as-evidence mapping. Add a row in the mapping table for Clause 7 mapping to the append-only journal/disclosure, and add a corresponding `SP["support (cl.7)"]` node in the diagram's `ISMS` subgraph.

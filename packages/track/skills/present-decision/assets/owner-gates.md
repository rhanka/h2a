# Pending owner gates

A GO, signature or decision required in a particular session must reach the owner there immediately.
Present the shortest complete stakes dossier (action/boundary, why this session, options and
consequences, recommendation, small diagram), then invoke that host's exposed native question tool
from `native-questions.md`. Do not defer the question until a status report or full HTML
polish; the short in-session dossier is sufficient for this immediate alert. A status-table row,
“type this in another session” or a Track decision card is not a raised native question.

Before a blocking tool call, bind the gate to an h2a objective loop or explicit Track pending item.
Record a stable gate id, human decision owner, responsible conductor, target instance/session,
UTC escalation deadline, reminder cadence/next reminder, close condition and question evidence.
Use agreed deadlines/cadence, otherwise remind every 15 minutes and escalate after 30 minutes.
Assign the reminder executor; storage alone does not schedule reminders. Keep the same record on
retries. These are follow-up deadlines, never consent inferred from elapsed time.
If recording a genuine gate fails, raise the native alert with that limitation immediately,
escalate the tracking repair and keep the action blocked; never claim an unsaved binding.

The requesting conductor verifies that a native question was accepted/displayed (gate id, tool,
session, timestamp, call/trace reference), including when the call blocks until answered.
An attempted/rejected call is not a raised question. Without that
evidence the question is `unverified`; delivery or `decision-raised:<id>` proves no native question.
Seek missing evidence through h2a immediately, re-raise a missing question at each due reminder,
and remind about an open question without stacking dialogs. Escalate unavailable tooling/evidence
immediately or an unanswered deadline. If no native tool is available, visibly show the limitation,
record `question-unavailable`, and relay the alert to a capable owner-facing session; that relay
cannot transfer a session-bound GO. Keep the blocked action pending and continue independent work.

Close only with an attributable owner answer in the required session and the required signature,
or explicit owner cancellation/supersession. Record rejection as rejection; deferral stays pending
with a new reminder. A peer signature, acknowledgement, timeout or completed loop is not approval.
Reuse existing authorization. For cross-session delivery use the h2a skill's current signed send
contract; its machine-readable `owner-gate` extension remains a specified next lot.

# Dossier self-audit

Fix failures and re-check before presenting. Missing evidence, criteria or review means
**Incomplete**: request the missing input, never final validation.

- [ ] **Title first:** subject and decision object in plain words; distinguishable among
  50 dossiers; one short line, no slogan/thesis. HTML `<title>` matches the visible title;
  small type (≤1.25rem), thin band, no hero.
- [ ] **Owner interest:** explains what changes for the owner and the risk they bear; preserves
  their intention. Never justify by agent inconvenience or false problems.
- [ ] **Reading budget:** declared total time fits the stakes; every question, including
  context, 2–4 short options and recommendation, is readable in 2 minutes (aim: at most 400 words).
- [ ] **Self-contained:** representative scenario, current behavior, affected people and constraints
  are understandable without links. Plain words; acronyms explained; no opaque codes masking meaning.
- [ ] **No hidden decisions:** every implied choice is explicit; no nested sub-options. Additional
  questions stand alone, with dependencies stated. Supporting evidence hides no choice or material risk.
- [ ] **Traceability:** dated/sourced literal owner request quoted; every demand maps to a question,
  option or criterion. Departures are explicit; each criterion is covered or is the decision asked.
- [ ] **Options and recommendation:** one-line owner consequence, cost/rollback and strongest honest case per option.
  Recommendation, strongest objection and overturning condition stated.
- [ ] **Evidence and bias:** decisive claims marked `[FACT]` with sources or `[JUDGMENT]`;
  assumptions/unknowns separate; brief failure scenario; presenter convenience separate from owner value.
- [ ] **Reviews:** independent Codex + Opus passes for complex design; disagreements surfaced;
  check risk/owner intent; avoid unnecessary reading.
- [ ] **Capture:** dossier with diagram before the native question tool; never a bare text question.
  If unavailable, explain and leave the decision pending. No fabricated `comprehension[]` or relay as decider.
